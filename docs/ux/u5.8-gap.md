  on today's product), the **data and sheet for the round** (a mock UAT dataset now; the spec of the

## 3. Decisions taken (most conservative option)

- **No budget is changed.** A journey a decision keeps over budget gets a capped, reviewed exception —
  never a higher budget, never a step hidden from the count.
- **Nothing is trimmed to make a number.** The claim's remedy stays the customer's choice (D-198), the
  job acceptance keeps its confirmation, the measurement basis stays a choice (D-182).
- **The mock UAT dataset is off by default**, appended after every existing seed, so no automated test
  meets it and no seeded id moves (tested).
- **T12's layers are checked on the real API** (`e2e/integration/downloads.spec.ts`), not on the
  mock's placeholder PDF.
