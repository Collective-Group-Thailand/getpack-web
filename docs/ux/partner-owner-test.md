# Partner portal — untrained owner test (P1–P10)

> A moderated test of the factory zone with the people it is for: factory **owners**, ideally
> 50+, who have **never been shown** the system. Asked for by the product owner (2026-10-09):
> "would an older owner understand it with nobody teaching him?" Same shape as
> `task-cards.md`: **Part A** is what the participant sees (Thai, one card at a time), **Part B**
> is the moderator's sheet. Consent and recording follow `consent-pdpa.md`; how to run a session
> follows `moderator-guide.md`.

- **Who:** 3–5 owners or planners of partner factories (UAT rehearsal factory accounts). At least
  two aged 50+, at least one who does the books himself. Nobody from the onboarding call.
- **Where:** their own office computer (Chrome) for P1–P9; their own phone for P3 and P5 again.
- **Data:** UAT factory with: 2 jobs waiting to accept, 1 job in production, 1 packed shipment,
  1 claim waiting for an answer, 1 billing statement sent, payout schedule with an advance and a
  retention, price list version 2 for corrugated boxes. Reset before each participant.
- **Rule for the moderator:** do not explain words, do not point. If asked, answer "ลองทำแบบที่
  คิดว่าใช่ได้เลยครับ". After 3 minutes stuck on one card, mark it failed and move on.

## Part B — moderator's sheet

| Card | Start | Done when | Watch for (log verbatim) | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Today (`/`), signed in as owner | names 2 of: jobs to accept, inspection, sample, claim, statement | reads the strip or the to-do list? scrolls past? | ≤ 30 s |
| P2 | Today | job accepted; said the ship-by date and the amount | finds the accept card summary or scrolls to the PO; hesitates at "รับงาน" | ≤ 90 s, no wrong job |
| P3 | Today | step completed with 1,000 / 0 | finds the job (search? list? QR?); understands "รับเข้า / ดี / เสีย" | ≤ 2 min |
| P4 | job page | issue raised, kind = material | looks for the issue form; the word "ปัญหา" | ≤ 90 s |
| P5 | Today | labels opened; dispatch confirmed | understands steps 1 → 2; own truck vs getpack carrier | ≤ 2 min |
| P6 | Today | dispute sent with a reason | reads what "ยอมรับ / ไม่ยอมรับ" lead to; finds photo upload | ≤ 2 min |
| P7 | Today | statement confirmed; said the total | VAT vs before-VAT confusion; "ใบกำกับภาษีของโรงงาน" | ≤ 2 min |
| P8 | Today | said the balance date and the retention amount | opens "คำในหน้านี้หมายถึงอะไร"? confuses advance / balance / retention | ≤ 2 min |
| P9 | Today | proposal sent with the one changed line | finds "แก้ราคา"; understands the effective date; "บันทึกไว้ก่อน" vs send | ≤ 4 min |
| P10 | Today | hours saved with Saturday closed, from next week | finds hours under the menu; the "มีผลตั้งแต่" date | ≤ 2 min |

**Log for every card:** done / done with help / failed · time · wrong turns (page opened that was
not on the way) · every word the participant asked about or read aloud twice · anything they said
was too small, too big or hard to see.

**After the ten cards (5 minutes):**
1. "ถ้าพรุ่งนี้ต้องใช้ระบบนี้คนเดียว มั่นใจแค่ไหน" (1 ไม่มั่นใจเลย – 5 มั่นใจมาก)
2. "ส่วนไหนที่ยากที่สุด"
3. "คำไหนที่ไม่เข้าใจ"
4. "ตัวหนังสือ/ปุ่ม เล็กหรือใหญ่ไปไหม ตรงไหน"

**Pass bar for the release:** every card done without help by ≥ 4 of 5 participants; median
confidence ≥ 4; no word asked about by more than one participant left unexplained. Each failure
becomes an issue labelled `partner-ux` with the card id and the participant's own words.
