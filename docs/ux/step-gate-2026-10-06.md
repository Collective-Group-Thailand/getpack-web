> **Audience:** whoever decides, after launch, which journeys pass the scorecard gate (D-217), and the
> moderator preparing the round. What the step counters (`pnpm e2e:journeys`, enforced) measure on
> the U5.8 branch, against the U5.0 baseline (`step-baseline-2026-09-29.md`), counted by
> `step-counting.md`. These are the **shortest paths that exist**; the moderated round measures what
> people actually do. **This is not a scorecard round**: no participant has used getpack yet, so
> completion, time, SEQ, SUS, mobile and screen-reader gates are still unmeasured.

exception** (`competitive-scorecard.md` §5). CI fails from now on when any of that changes for the
worse: a journey over budget without an exception, above its ceiling, not walkable, or with a
required field the scorecard does not list — and when an excepted journey comes back within budget
while its exception is still listed.
