## 4. Assumptions (also in PROGRESS)

- U5.0 runs in `../packio-web-u1` on `feat/u5.0-baseline`, cut from `main` after U-F2 was merged.
- The funnel events stay under legitimate interest (not consent-gated, as `consent.ts` explains); replay is consent-gated because it records what people see and do.
- The SUS text is our own Thai rendering; a validated published Thai SUS may replace it if the business prefers — scores stay comparable only if every round uses the same text.
